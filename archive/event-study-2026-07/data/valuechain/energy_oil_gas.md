## Context

## Non-Obvious Indirect Dependent Sectors (the valuable part of this research)

**Airlines** — fuel 20-30%+ of opex. Hedge posture is THE tradeable fact:
| Company | Hedged? |
|---|---|
| Delta (DAL) | Unhedged but owns Monroe Energy refinery (~75% of fuel needs) |
| United (UAL), American (AAL) | Fully unhedged (exited ~10yrs ago) |
| Southwest (LUV) | Unhedged as of Q2 2025 (fully terminated hedge book) |
| Ryanair | ~80% hedged FY26-27 ($668/t vs ~$1,300/t spot) — best-protected large carrier |
| Carnival (CCL) | 0% hedged |
| Royal Caribbean (RCL) | ~60% hedged |
| Norwegian (NCLH) | ~51% hedged |

**Chemicals/plastics**: US ethane crackers (Dow, LyondellBasell, Westlake, Huntsman, Eastman) have $200-300/ton structural cost advantage over Asian/European naphtha crackers. When oil spikes but gas stays cheap (oil-supply-specific shock like Hormuz), ratio widens further — US margins expand vs Europe/Asia. 2-3 hop trade: oil shock → naphtha cost spike (Europe/Asia) → Dow/LyondellBasell margin expansion + global ethylene/PE price rises → downstream plastics/packaging cost pass-through.

**Shipping/trucking**: FedEx/UPS fuel surcharges are index-linked with ~1wk lag (EIA diesel/jet fuel indices). **Dual dynamic**: chokepoint disruption (Red Sea, Hormuz) forces Cape of Good Hope reroutes (+10-14 days) → shrinks effective vessel supply → bullish for tanker/freight rates even as it's bearish for fuel-consuming transports. Scorpio Tankers (STNG), Frontline (FRO) benefit; equity re-rating can LAG the freight-rate spike (relative-value opportunity). War-risk insurance for Hormuz transits spiked 0.25%→10% of hull value during 2026 crisis ($3-8M per large tanker transit).

**Agriculture**: nitrogen fertilizer (CF, Nutrien) is a NATURAL GAS story not oil — Middle East gas disruption pushed urea +31-46%. Mosaic (potash/phosphate-weighted) less exposed to nitrogen rally. Diesel is the direct oil linkage (+32% since 2020 for farms) — double-squeeze with financing costs (+73%) depressing farm income; hits Deere/AGCO demand even as it helps CF/NTR margins.

**Utilities**: US power generation is NOT oil-driven (gas/coal/nuclear/renewables) — natural gas (Henry Hub) is the real linkage, loosely correlated with oil. Broad Middle East shock can lift both via risk premia + LNG-export linkage. ERCOT/PJM prices spiked 22-81% in 2026 crisis. Vistra (VST)/NRG (NRG) = merchant generators with direct upside leverage (gas price takers, power price beneficiaries). Constellation (CEG) more nuclear-heavy = "best of both worlds" (low fuel-cost exposure, high output-price beta).

**Autos**: elasticity of demand for gas vehicles ≈ -0.53. 2022 spike → EV consideration jumped 17.5%→25.1% (Feb→Mar). 2026 spike shows WEAKER response — hybrids (not full EVs) capturing most growth, "Tesla-benefit" thesis weaker than 2022. Ford pivoted toward hybrids/PHEVs, canceled next-gen large EV truck platform.

**Other 2-3 hop chains**: Cruise lines same hedged/unhedged pattern as airlines (CCL most exposed). Plastics/packaging: naphtha = 70-90% of resin cost, $10/bbl oil rise → 5-10% plastic price rise in a month → eventually hits FMCG COGS (packaging = 10-25% of product cost). Gold (+64% 2025, geopolitical risk = 8-12% of return) and defense stocks (+36-55% 2025) as macro hedges/direct expressions of the same risk factor. Rates: 3% oil-supply surprise moved 2yr yields ~4.5bps in early 2026 (3x pre-2021 sensitivity) — TIPS breakevens are the cleanest real-time gauge.
