## Value Chain Structure

Raw materials/IP (silicon, rare earths, lithium, cobalt; ARM licenses CPU arch) → Components (chip design/fab, memory, displays, batteries, sensors) → Contract assembly/EMS-ODM (China/Taiwan/India/Vietnam) → Brand (design/OS/marketing) → Retail/distribution (carriers, big-box, e-commerce) → Consumer.

**Semiconductor fab**: TSMC 72.3% of foundry market Q1 2026 (Samsung 6.5%, SMIC 5.1%, UMC 3.9%). TSMC customer mix 2026: Apple ~22-25%, Nvidia ~11% (Nvidia set to overtake Apple as #1 customer per Jan 2026 CNBC report — AI buildout now rivals smartphones), Broadcom ~11-15%, MediaTek ~9-10%, Qualcomm ~8%, AMD ~7%.

**Displays**: Samsung Display 41-48% global OLED revenue (52%+ of smartphone OLED Q4 2025), LG Display #2 (14%→21% 2024→2025), BOE #3 (~14%). Samsung Display is Apple's largest OLED supplier AND exclusive supplier for Apple's first foldable iPhone.

**Batteries**: ATL (CATL-affiliated) primary iPhone/Xiaomi cell supplier; Samsung SDI anchors Galaxy; LG Energy mid-range; CATL piloting solid-state for smartphones.

**Sensors**: Sony dominates image sensors (~43-60% share depending on period, followed by Samsung System LSI, OmniVision). STMicro — ToF/proximity/MEMS.

**Memory**: DRAM Q1 2026: Samsung 38%, SK Hynix 29%, Micron 22%. HBM (AI-critical): SK Hynix ~58-62%. Global memory shortage made Samsung Apple's lead LPDDR supplier (60-70% of iPhone 17 lineup) — DRAM tightness flagged as 2026 smartphone headwind.

**Assembly**: Foxconn (largest iPhone assembler, also ~40% of AI server rack assembly — dominant Nvidia GB200/GB300 builder), Pegatron (Tata Electronics now majority owner of India unit), Luxshare (gaining share, expanding in Vietnam not India). Apple targeting majority of US-bound iPhones from India by end of 2026. Vietnam's overall smartphone production share slipped 60%→45-50% as India/Indonesia gain.

**Brands**: Apple (240.6M iPhones shipped 2025, +7%), Samsung (led Q1 2026 with 21.2% vs Apple 21.0% — razor-thin), Xiaomi #3 (~11%), Amazon/Sony/Google/Microsoft cross-layer players.

**Retail**: Carriers (AT&T/Verizon/T-Mobile) = >75% of US iPhone sales; Apple's own channel down to 16% (from 20% in 2023). Best Buy: Computing & Mobile = 45% of revenue.

## Key vertical-integration nuances
- **Samsung**: component supplier to Apple (OLED, DRAM) AND direct brand competitor AND own captive assembler (Vietnam) — touches most layers of any single company.
- **Sony**: brand (PlayStation) AND dominant image-sensor supplier to Apple/Samsung/Chinese OEMs.
- **Broadcom**: RF chip supplier to Apple AND fabless chip designer (Google TPUs) manufactured at TSMC.
- **Foxconn**: spans consumer smartphone EMS (Apple) AND AI-server EMS (Nvidia) — bellwether for both cycles.
