# Aerospace & Defense Value Chain: Chokepoints & Dependency Map

## 1. Critical Chokepoint Suppliers
- **Titanium**: Pre-2022, Russia's VSMPO-AVISMA supplied ~80% of Boeing's and ~60% of Airbus's titanium. Boeing suspended purchases March 2022; Airbus phased out through 2023 but EU dropped VSMPO from sanctions after lobbying — VSMPO still shipped ~$345M to Western buyers in 2023 (partial derisking only). Western alternatives: TIMET/Precision Castparts (Berkshire), Howmet Aerospace (HWM).
- **Forgings/castings duopoly**: Howmet (HWM) + Precision Castparts (Berkshire) — ~$10B market, chronically capacity-constrained (2025 PCC production delays gave HWM pricing power).
- **Jet engines** (3-way oligopoly): GE Aerospace + Safran/CFM JV ~55% (narrowbody + 787/777X), RTX/Pratt & Whitney ~26% (narrowbody GTF only), Rolls-Royce ~18% (widebody). An A320neo/GTF problem hits RTX+Airbus not Boeing/GE.
- **Spirit AeroSystems resolved (Dec 2025)**: Boeing completed acquisition of Spirit's Boeing-related ops (737 fuselages etc.); Airbus acquired Spirit's Airbus-related sites. Removes a standalone single-point-of-failure ticker — risk now inside BA/Airbus directly.
- **Avionics**: Honeywell Aerospace in ~90% of in-service aircraft, ~70% of military — **spinning off from Honeywell (HON) as standalone public co, shares distributed ~June 29, 2026** (~$17B revenue). Collins Aerospace (part of RTX) also dominant. TransDigm (TDG) = sole-source aftermarket monopolist.
- **Rare earths/magnets**: China's dual-use export licensing (since April 2025, tightened Oct 2025, enforcement delay to **Nov 2026**) explicitly excludes defense/aerospace from general licenses. MP Materials (MP) = primary US alternative.

## 2. Value Chain Structure
Raw materials (titanium: VSMPO/TIMET/ATI, rare earths: MP Materials) → Forgings/castings/structures (Howmet, Precision Castparts, Spirit now inside BA/Airbus) → Engines/avionics/subsystems (GE+Safran/CFM, RTX/Pratt&Whitney+Collins, Rolls-Royce, Honeywell Aerospace, TransDigm) → Prime contractors (Boeing BA, Lockheed Martin LMT, Northrop Grumman NOC, RTX, General Dynamics GD, L3Harris LHX; private: SpaceX, Anduril, General Atomics) → End customers (DoD budget, NATO/allied governments, airlines).

## 3. Downstream Dependents & Government-Contract Chokepoint
- **RTX**: triple-chokepoint name (Pratt & Whitney engines + Collins avionics + Raytheon missiles).
- **Boeing**: commercial titanium/engine exposure + now full Spirit structures risk in-house + defense (F-47/NGAD win March 2025).
- **Lockheed Martin**: F-35 single-program concentration (~27% of revenue) — more exposed to political/pricing statements than raw-material chokepoints.
- **Government-as-chokepoint**: Oct 2025 shutdown delayed contract awards but muted stock impact (~-0.01% avg) given multiyear backlogs/60-90 day liquidity buffers.
- **Jan 7, 2026 executive order** ("Prioritizing the Warfighter") restricting buybacks/dividends/exec pay at underperforming primes caused same-day broad selloff (BA, RTX, GD, LMT, NOC), partially reversed same day by $1.5T FY2027 defense budget announcement — clean whipsaw example.

## 4. Historical Bottleneck/Shock Events
1. **Dec 12 & 22, 2016**: Trump tweets on F-35 cost/Boeing F/A-18 alternative — LMT -4-5% (~$4B market cap) first tweet, -$1.2B more on follow-up. Template single-tweet defense-stock case.
2. **Mar 10-Nov 18, 2020**: 737 MAX grounding (20 months) after Ethiopian 302 crash — cascaded to CFM/GE, Spirit fuselage cuts.
3. **Mar-Apr 2020**: COVID — Boeing shut Puget Sound (~80% of output) + Charleston 787 line; Airbus halted EU plants.
4. **Feb 24, 2022+**: Russia invades Ukraine — Boeing suspends VSMPO titanium; broad NATO rearmament re-rating lifted LMT/NOC/RTX/GD + Rheinmetall/BAE for years (largest sustained defense re-rating in this dataset).
5. **Jul-Sep 2023**: RTX/Pratt & Whitney GTF powder-metal contamination — ~1,200 engines recalled, ~$3B Q3 2023 charge (total $6-7B), RTX -8% on Sep 11 2023 disclosure. Groundings peaked ~650 jets 2024, still elevated 2025-2026 (300-360 day shop turnaround).
6. **Oct 2025 shutdown**: delayed awards, muted stock impact due to backlogs.
7. **Jan 7, 2026 executive order**: see §3.

## 5. Current Risk Points (mid-2026)
- Titanium dependency not fully resolved — renewed sanctions/export ban/escalation could still hit BA/Airbus with little warning.
- China rare-earth dual-use licensing truce enforcement delay expires **November 2026** — hard deadline for renewed defense magnet/component shortages.
- Castings/forgings duopoly (HWM/PCC) capacity-constrained — disruption at one concentrates risk/pricing power at the other.
- Honeywell Aerospace spinoff (~June 29, 2026) — new standalone avionics ticker to track.
- Executive-order compliance regime is an ongoing policy overhang — recurring fast-moving catalyst across whole prime cohort simultaneously.
- Golden Dome ($151B SHIELD contract), F-47/NGAD, Sentinel ICBM, CCA (Northrop/Anduril/General Atomics frontrunners) — major pending 2026 award decisions likely to produce idiosyncratic jumps.
- RTX's GTF fleet issue still working through system into 2026 (slow-burn recurring guidance revisions).
