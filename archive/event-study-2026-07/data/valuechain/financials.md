## 1. Critical Policy/Rate Chokepoints

**Federal Reserve**: FOMC meets 8x/year. As of mid-2026, target range held 3.50-3.75% since March 18, 2026 meeting. Next meeting July 28-29, 2026. Dot plot moves rate-sensitive sectors even absent an actual rate change. Powell press-conference language itself market-moving (historical intraday 1-2% S&P swings on hawkish/dovish phrasing). Most NIM-exposed: JPM, BAC, WFC, C + regionals (PNC, USB, TFC, RF, KEY).

**Basel III Endgame**: NOT finalized as of mid-2026. Original 2023 proposal (would've raised large-bank capital 19-30%) collapsed under pushback. March 19, 2026: capital-neutral-to-lower re-proposal issued; comment period closed June 18, 2026; finalization expected late 2026, phase-in 2027. Applies to 8 US G-SIBs: JPM, BAC, C, WFC, GS, MS, BK, STT.

**Credit rating agencies**: All three (S&P 2011, Fitch 2023, Moody's May 16 2026) have now stripped US of top-tier sovereign rating — first time in history. Market reaction magnitude declining over cycles (2011: -6.6% single day S&P; 2023/2026: "largely flat"). Sovereign downgrades now a fade/non-event; bank-specific rating actions move names far more than the index. MCO/SPGI are toll-booth plays on all US debt issuance (ratings duopoly).

**Payment rails/clearinghouses as single points of failure**:
- Visa/Mastercard: ~90% of card processing outside China (duopoly, 55-65%+ operating margins). 2018 Visa outage: 10hrs, 5.2M failed transactions.
- Fedwire: sole USD real-time gross settlement (>$3T/day). Feb 24, 2021: ~4hr all-services outage from operational error, halted crypto exchange settlements nationwide.
- SWIFT: no scaled alternative to cross-border messaging. Feb-Mar 2022: 7 Russian banks cut off (Sberbank/Gazprombank exempted to protect energy flows) — ruble fell ~30%, CBR doubled rates to 20%.
- DTCC: central counterparty for vast majority of US securities trades — self-acknowledged single point of failure.

## 2. Value Chain Structure (bank transmission)

Fed policy → wholesale funding costs (immediate) → deposit costs (lagged, several quarters even after hikes stop — cumulative deposit beta reached 49.3% by mid-2024, a year after last 2023 hike) → NIM → lending/credit standards → mortgage/C&I/credit card/auto loan pricing.

**GSIB/money-center (JPM, BAC, WFC, C, GS, MS)**: buffered by non-interest income (trading, IB fees, wealth mgmt). Goldman Q1 2026 IB fees $2.84B, equities trading $5.33B record. JPM asset/wealth mgmt $4.8T AUM (+16% YoY). JPM market cap now exceeds BAC+C+WFC combined.

**Regional banks (PNC, TFC, USB, RF, KEY, ZION, CMA, MTB, FITB, HBAN)**: lack this diversification — Regions Financial non-interest income only ~35% of revenue (vs GSIBs where trading/IB/wealth can exceed half). Far more exposed to deposit flight, wholesale funding cost spikes, duration mismatch.

**March 2023 SVB/Signature stress divergence**: XLF -5.9% vs KRE -12.5% (Mar 9-10). March 13: First Republic -61.8%, Western Alliance -47%, PacWest -45%, KeyCorp -27%, Zions -26%. Banks flagged for HTM/AFS duration risk: CMA, ZION, WAL, KEY, TFC (34 banks >$1B assets w/ unrealized losses ≥50% of CET1 as of recent quarter, up from 12 a year earlier, concentrated in $10-200B asset range).
