# Automotive Industry Value Chain: Chokepoints & Disruption Mapping

## 1. Critical Chokepoint Suppliers
- **Semiconductors**: oligopoly — Infineon (IFX, ~13% share, power electronics/EV inverters), NXP (NXPI, ~10%, V2X/security/MCUs), Texas Instruments (TXN, ~8%), Renesas (6723.T, ~7%, MCUs — historically 30-40% of global auto MCU supply), STMicro (STM). Long qualification cycles + concentrated back-end packaging/test in Malaysia/Taiwan/China = hard to substitute quickly.
- **Battery/EV supply chain**: CATL (300750.SZ, ~40-45% global installs early 2026) + BYD (~14%) = over half the market. LG Energy Solution (373220.KS, ~9%), Panasonic (6752.T, ~3.4%, Tesla-dependent). China refines >50% of lithium, 30%+ of nickel/cobalt processing despite mining <5% domestically.
- **Wire harnesses (Ukraine)**: ~7% of all EU auto-component imports from Ukraine pre-war; Leoni (Stryi, Kolomyia plants) and Fujikura (Lviv). Bulky, custom-per-model, can't be stockpiled/air-freighted — a physical interruption halts assembly almost immediately.
- **Nexperia (emerging)**: Dutch, owned by China's Wingtech; makes basic discretes used in nearly every ECU. 2025 Dutch government seizure + Chinese export-license retaliation.

## 2. Value Chain Structure
Raw materials → components → Tier-1/2 (Bosch, Continental, Magna, ZF, Denso, Aptiv, Leoni, Fujikura) → OEM assembly (Ford F, GM, Toyota TM, Stellantis STLA, Tesla TSLA, Honda HMC, VW VWAGY, Nissan NSANY, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai/Kia, BYD vertically integrated) → dealers.

## 3. Downstream Dependents (direct vs indirect)

| Chokepoint | Direct (1 hop) | Indirect (2+ hops) |
|---|---|---|
| Renesas/NXP MCU shortage | Toyota, Honda, Nissan | Ford/GM via shared Tier-1s |
| Ukraine wire harnesses | VW, BMW, Mercedes, Porsche, Ford (EU plants) | Suppliers to those OEMs |
| CATL/LG/Panasonic disruption | Tesla, Ford/Stellantis (SK On/LG JVs), GM (Ultium) | Dealers/lenders, charging network operators |
| China lithium/cobalt export controls | CATL, BYD, LG Energy, Panasonic | Nearly entire global EV fleet OEMs |
| Nexperia discretes | Toyota, Honda, Nissan, EU OEMs | Tier-1 ECU makers (Bosch, Continental, Denso) |

## 4. Historical Bottleneck/Shock Events
1. **March 11, 2011 — Tohoku earthquake/tsunami**: Renesas supplied ~40% of world auto MCUs; Toyota Japan output -63% in March, -74% April; Honda -81% April; Toyota Q1 net income -77%.
2. **Feb 2021 — Texas winter storm Uri**: NXP's 2 Austin fabs (~30% of NXP global footprint) fully shut Feb 15; Samsung/Infineon Austin fabs also halted. NXP resumed March 11.
3. **March 19-22, 2021 — Renesas Naka fire**: 11-17 fab tools damaged; two-thirds of affected line was automotive. Recovery took over a month.
4. **Aug 2021 — Malaysia COVID lockdowns**: Handles ~13% of global chip test/assembly. Toyota suspended 14 plants; Nissan shut Smyrna TN for 2 weeks. 2021 industry-wide cost: ~$100-110B lost revenue; Ford ~$2.5B hit, GM ~$1.5-2B.
5. **Feb-Mar 2022 — Russia invades Ukraine**: Leoni/Fujikura Ukraine plants shut ~1 week; VW, Porsche, BMW, Mercedes, Ford cut German production within days (harnesses can't be stockpiled).
6. **Sep-Nov 2025 — Nexperia crisis**: US BIS Entity List + Dutch seizure under Goods Availability Act + Chinese export licensing retaliation on Dongguan packaging. Toyota, Honda, Nissan named as directly exposed; unresolved into 2026.

## 5. Current Risk Points (2026)
- **Section 232 auto tariffs**: 25% on passenger vehicles/parts since April 3, 2025 (non-USMCA-exempt). USMCA requires 75% regional value content.
- **China EV tariffs & rare earth export controls**: 100% US tariff on Chinese EVs; China's April 2025 rare earth export controls threaten non-Chinese EV/motor production as retaliation.
- **China EV overcapacity**: 5-10M vehicles/year overcapacity; BYD now #1 international EV brand; ~1-in-10 cars sold in Europe now Chinese brand. GM/Ford pulled back EV investment amid losses.
- **Battery JV unwind risk**: GM selling down Ultium Cells stake, Ford dissolved BlueOval SK + canceled $6.5B LG Energy deal, Samsung SDI/Stellantis unwinding StarPlus Energy JV — signals capital-allocation risk at GM/Ford/Stellantis.

**Trading heuristic**: triage chokepoint news by (a) directly-hit company (fab/mine/plant), (b) OEMs sourcing directly (1 hop, often named in article), (c) OEMs/suppliers sharing the same Tier-1 or refining/packaging geography (2+ hops, rarely named explicitly — highest-value, slowest-to-price opportunity).
