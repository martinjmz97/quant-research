# Pharmaceutical & Biotech Value Chain: Chokepoints and Dependencies

## 1. Critical Chokepoint Suppliers
- **API manufacturing**: China + India = ~2/3 of world output. China ~20% of global API production but ~80% of supply chain for certain generic inputs; India sources 70-80% of its APIs from China (penicillin 95.8%, ciprofloxacin 99.6%, paracetamol 91% from China). US FDA-registered API sites: US 22%, India 21%, China 20%, EU 19%.
- **CDMOs**: top 5 — Lonza (~12% share), Thermo Fisher/Patheon, Catalent (now Novo Holdings-owned), Samsung Biologics (largest capacity, 845,000L), WuXi Biologics — collectively only ~30% of a fragmented market.
- **WuXi (AppTec/Biologics/XDC)** — acute chokepoint + political risk. Top-5 CDMO/CRO, key GLP-1 peptide supplier (feeds Eli Lilly's tirzepatide). Dec 18, 2025: BIOSECURE Act enacted via NDAA (doesn't name WuXi directly; OMB must publish "biotechnology companies of concern" list by Dec 2026). June 8, 2026: Pentagon added WuXi AppTec to 1260H "Chinese Military Company" list → automatic BCC status under BIOSECURE; stock fell ~3.7% HK; WuXi sued Pentagon. WuXi Biologics NOT on the June 2026 list.

## 2. Value Chain Structure
Raw materials/KSMs (China-heavy) → API manufacturing (China ~20%, India ~29-38% of essential APIs) → CDMOs (Lonza, Catalent, Samsung Biologics, Thermo Fisher, WuXi) → Branded/generic drugmakers (Pfizer, Merck, J&J, Eli Lilly, Novartis, BMS, AbbVie, AstraZeneca, Amgen, Roche + generics Teva/Viatris/Sandoz) → Wholesale distributors (McKesson, Cencora, Cardinal Health = 90-98% of US wholesale) → Pharmacies/PBMs (CVS Caremark, Express Scripts, Optum Rx = ~80% of US prescription claims) → Patients/payers.

## 3. Downstream/Indirect Dependents
- China-API-dependent generics makers (Teva, Viatris/Sandoz, Fresenius Kabi, Amphastar, Hikma) — thin margins, outsized exposure to export restrictions.
- US biotechs using WuXi as sole-source CDMO face costly multi-year tech transfer to alternatives (Lonza, Samsung Biologics, Fujifilm Diosynth, Catalent) if BIOSECURE bites.
- Large Medicare Part D-exposed blockbusters (BMS/Pfizer-Eliquis, Merck-Keytruda, J&J-Stelara, Lilly-Jardiance, Amgen-Enbrel) directly hit by IRA Medicare Negotiation (first 10 drugs effective 2026, min 38% off 2023 price).
- Tariff exposure: ex-US manufacturing heavy firms (Novartis, Roche, EU/Japan/Korea pharma) vs. US-heavy (Vertex 100% US, AbbVie, BMS) more insulated.

## 4. Historical Bottleneck/Shock Events
1. **Feb-Mar 2020**: COVID China API export restrictions (26 products, ~10% of pharma exports) before reversing.
2. **Feb 2023**: Akorn Pharmaceuticals bankruptcy — Ch.7, abrupt shutdown of 4 US plants, recall of ~70 generics.
3. **2023**: FDA shuts Intas Pharmaceuticals plant (India) over falsified QC records — supplied ~50% of US cisplatin, triggered chemo-drug shortage.
4. **Dec 2024**: Novo Holdings completes $16.5B Catalent acquisition + Novo Nordisk buys 3 Catalent fill-finish sites for $11B to secure GLP-1 capacity.
5. **Sep-Oct 2024**: Hurricane Helene floods Baxter's North Cove NC plant (~60% of US IV-fluid supply) — nationwide shortage.
6. **Sep 25, 2025 – Apr 2026**: Trump pharma tariff campaign — 100% tariff threat on branded/patented imports (exempts firms "breaking ground" on US plants). Sept 30: Pfizer struck MFN pricing deal for 3-yr exemption. Apr 2026: Section 232 tariffs formalized (UK 10%, EU/Japan/Korea/Switzerland 15%, others up to 100%). Pharma pledged >$270B US manufacturing investment.
7. **Dec 18 2025 → June 8 2026**: BIOSECURE Act enacted → WuXi AppTec added to 1260H list (see §1).

## 5. Current Risk Points
- China API/CDMO dependency: any export control/tariff/FDA warning letter can reprice generics/API-linked names.
- WuXi/BIOSECURE overhang: OMB's BCC list (due Dec 2026), further 1260H additions, WuXi's lawsuit outcome, FAR rulemaking (prohibitions ~2027).
- Section 232 pharma tariffs: differentiated by country and "onshoring commitment" status.
- Drug pricing: IRA expansion, CMS proposed permanent negotiation framework (June 2026) effective 2029.
- Distribution/PBM oligopoly antitrust risk (McKesson/Cencora/Cardinal Health; CVS Caremark/Express Scripts/Optum Rx).
- GLP-1 capacity: officially "resolved" shortages but structurally tight through 2027-2028; fragile given WuXi-linked peptide inputs.
