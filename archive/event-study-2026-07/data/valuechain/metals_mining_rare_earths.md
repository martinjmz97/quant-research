# Metals & Mining / Rare Earths & Critical Minerals: Value Chain & Chokepoint Research

## 1. Critical Chokepoint Suppliers
- **Rare earth refining (the real chokepoint, not mining)**: China mines ~60-70% but refines/processes ~90% of world supply; ~99% of heavy rare earth (HREE) refining. Even non-Chinese-mined ore typically must be refined in China.
- **MP Materials (MP)**: primary US pure-play, Mountain Pass CA mine. DoD's largest shareholder since July 2025 ($400M preferred equity, 10-yr offtake 7,000MT/yr NdPr magnets at guaranteed floor $110/kg). Nov 2025: MP/Pentagon/Saudi Maaden JV for Saudi refinery.
- **Gallium**: China ~98% of global supply. **Germanium**: similarly China-dominated (fiber optics, night-vision, defense semis). **Graphite**: China >60% flake, ~80% synthetic; 90-93% of Korean/Japanese anode inputs from China. **Cobalt**: DRC ~70% mining, China dominates refining; DRC export suspension Feb 2025. **Nickel**: Indonesia dominates mining but Chinese JVs (CATL) control processing. **Copper**: Chile (Codelco) + Peru (Southern Copper, Freeport-McMoRan's Cerro Verde) concentration.

## 2. Value Chain Structure
Mining (China, US/MP Mountain Pass, Australia/Lynas, Chile/Peru copper, DRC cobalt, Indonesia nickel) → Refining/Separation (China-dominated chokepoint) → Magnet/Component Manufacturing (~90% China; MP "10-X" and Lynas Seadrift TX are Western attempts) → End products (EVs, wind turbines, defense — F-35 has 900+ lbs rare earth materials, Virginia-class subs 9,200+ lbs; Lockheed largest US samarium-cobalt magnet consumer).

## 3. Downstream/Indirect Dependents
**Negatively exposed**: Lockheed Martin, RTX, Northrop Grumman, BAE (defense primes — DoD rule requires zero-China-content certification by Jan 1, 2027); Ford/GM/Tesla (motors); Qorvo/Skyworks/Coherent/Wolfspeed/ON Semi (GaAs/GaN defense electronics); GE Vernova/Vestas/Siemens Gamesa (wind).
**Beneficiaries**: MP Materials (+250% in 2025), Lynas Rare Earths (ASX:LYC, Seadrift TX facility DoD-funded $258M), USA Rare Earth, Freeport-McMoRan/Southern Copper (copper).

## 4. Historical Bottleneck/Shock Events
1. **Sept 2010**: China rare earth restrictions on Japan (Senkaku dispute) — prices rose ~10x within a year.
2. **July 2023 (eff. Aug 2023)**: China gallium/germanium export licensing — exports collapsed 6,876kg→227kg (Jul→Oct); prices +68%.
3. **Oct 2023 (eff. Dec 2023)**: China graphite export permits — hit Korean/Japanese battery anode supply.
4. **Dec 2024**: China gallium/germanium/antimony functional ban on US — ~20 US companies (~$8T market cap) flagged exposed.
5. **April 4, 2025**: China heavy rare earth licensing controls (7 REEs) — magnet exports fell ~75% in 2 months. Triggered **Ford's Chicago Explorer plant shutdowns (May-Jul 2025)**.
6. **Oct 9, 2025**: China's sweeping export controls incl. extraterritorial "0.1% de minimis rule" (any product w/ ≥0.1% Chinese REE content needs Beijing license), eff. Dec 1 2025. MP Materials +8% on re-shoring bet.
7. **Oct 30, 2025**: Trump-Xi Busan truce — 1-yr suspension of Oct 9 controls for tariff cuts (57%→47%) + soybean purchases. April 2025 controls remain largely in force.
8. **June 22, 2026**: China sanctions MP Materials + USA Rare Earth directly (retaliation for Pentagon's ~80-entity list add). MP stock fell 13.4% over following month but muted immediate reaction (already seen as "de-risked").

## 5. Current Risk Points (July 2026)
- **Truce expires ~Oct/Nov 2026** — scheduled event risk. April 2025 licensing regime never fully suspended.
- DoD Jan 1, 2027 zero-China-content certification deadline — contractor compliance scramble expected H2 2026.
- China now directly sanctioning Western re-shoring champions (MP, USAR) — new escalation vector.
- Cobalt/DRC quota tightness into 2026 (separate chokepoint).
- Nickel/Indonesia entangled with Chinese midstream investment — "Indonesia" ≠ genuine diversification.
