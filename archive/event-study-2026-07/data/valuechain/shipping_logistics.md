# Shipping & Logistics Value Chain: Chokepoint Map for Cross-Industry Disruption Trading

## 1. Critical Chokepoint Geography
- **Suez Canal**: ~12-15% of global trade. Ever Given (2021) showed a single vessel can shut ~$9-10bn/day for 6 days.
- **Red Sea/Bab-el-Mandeb**: Houthi attacks since Nov 2023 collapsed container traffic; Feb 2026 flare-up, June 2026 ceasefire/MOU — but carriers have NOT structurally returned. Shanghai-Rotterdam transit now "firmly established" at 40-45 days via Cape of Good Hope (was 28-32 via Suez) — durable, not transient.
- **Panama Canal**: 2023-2024 El Niño drought cut transits ~38→24/day at worst. Restored to full 50-ft draft by mid-2025 (33.84/day Jan 2026, still below 36-38 max). NOAA flags possible El Niño return mid-2026; permanent fix (Rio Indo dam) not ready until 2032.
- **Strait of Malacca**: busiest chokepoint — ~22-25% of global seaborne trade, ~23.2M bpd oil (29% of maritime oil flows), 40% of global LNG. China >80% of oil imports, Japan ~80%, Korea ~70% transit here.
- **Port congestion (2026)**: Shanghai/Ningbo ~3-day wait; Rotterdam 7-day export dwell — described as 4-year congestion high, Asian/European hub delays now feeding each other.
- **Carrier concentration**: Ocean Alliance (CMA CGM/COSCO/Evergreen/OOCL) 28.4%, Gemini Cooperation (Maersk+Hapag-Lloyd, Feb 2025) 21.6%, MSC (independent) 20.6%, Premier Alliance ~12%. Alliances+MSC = ~82% of global capacity — 3-4 entities can move the entire market by rerouting.

## 2. Value Chain Structure
Manufacturers (Foxconn, TSMC, Asia OEMs) → Freight forwarders (Kuehne+Nagel, DSV, DB Schenker, Expeditors EXPD, C.H. Robinson CHRW) → Container lines (Maersk, Hapag-Lloyd HLAG, ZIM, COSCO; MSC/CMA CGM private) → Ports (APM Terminals, PSA, DP World) → Inland rail (Union Pacific UNP, BNSF/Berkshire, Norfolk Southern NSC, CSX — UP/NS pending transcontinental merger targeted 2027) → Trucking (J.B. Hunt JBHT, Knight-Swift KNX, Old Dominion ODFL) → Distribution/3PL (Prologis PLD) → Retailers/OEMs. FedEx/UPS also run ocean freight-forwarding arms = secondary chokepoint exposure.

## 3. Downstream Transmission Lag (the key exploitable inefficiency)
1. **Freight rates/spot markets**: react within days (fastest signal).
2. **Carrier equity/earnings guidance**: re-rates within weeks — Maersk raised 2024 EBITDA guidance 4x as Red Sea diversions cut capacity/raised rates.
3. **Freight forwarders/3PLs/railroads**: 2-6 week lag as booking patterns shift.
4. **Retailer/manufacturer inventory & margin commentary**: 1-2 QUARTER lag. Restoration Hardware disclosed **$40M Q4 revenue hit** from Red Sea delays; Steve Madden built in 20-25bp gross margin hit; Adidas order bookings shifted Q1→Q2.
5. **Consumer CPI**: ~6 months to fully feed through — too slow for equity alpha, useful for macro positioning only.

**Trading implication**: tradeable window sits between step 2 (days) and step 4 (1-2 quarters). Auto/electronics names especially exposed INTRA-quarter since production lines halt (not just retail shelves) when a component is delayed — auto-parts container costs rose >40% since late 2024. Retailers that pre-built inventory post-2021 COVID lesson dampened 2023-24 Red Sea impact — inventory-buffer disclosures in 10-Ks are a moderating variable.

## 4. Historical Events
| Event | Date | Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| Ever Given/Suez | Mar 23-29, 2021 | ~$9bn/day disrupted, Maersk ~$89M loss, WTI/Brent +4% |
| LA/Long Beach congestion | 2021-2022 | Vessel wait hit 25 days (Nov 2021); Adidas inventory shortages; retailers over-built "safety stock" → 2022-23 destocking drag |
| Red Sea Houthi attacks begin | Nov 2023+ | Mass diversion to Cape of Good Hope (+10-14 days); Maersk raised 2024 guidance 4x |
| Panama drought | 2023-early 2024 | Transits ~halved at trough; elevated costs, rail/land-bridge incentivized |
| Red Sea pause→resume→ceasefire | Nov 2025→Feb 28 2026→mid-June 2026 | Traffic still not normalized as of May 2026 |
| Port congestion resurgence | Early-mid 2026 | 4-year congestion high across Shanghai/Ningbo/Rotterdam/Antwerp simultaneously |

## 5. Current Risk Points (July 2026)
- Red Sea ceasefire (June 14, 2026) holding but fragile — reported attack July 5, 2026. Any Iran-Israel-US flare-up is fastest trigger for renewed attacks.
- Panama at full draft/near-max throughput but El Niño return possible mid-2026; permanent fix not until 2032.
- Global port congestion at 4-year high independent of any single chokepoint — system has little slack, a fresh shock would compound not absorb.
- Strait of Malacca: no acute disruption but flagged as rising watch item given Hormuz-adjacent tensions.

## Bottom line for the trading model
Build event-detection around 3 chokepoints (Suez/Red Sea, Panama, Malacca) + a congestion-index layer (Shanghai/Ningbo/Rotterdam dwell times). Tag: (a) carrier tickers (MAERSK-B, HLAG, ZIM, COSCO) for T+0 to T+2wk, (b) rail/trucking/3PL (UNP, NSC, CHRW, EXPD, JBHT) for T+2 to T+6wk, (c) retail/auto/electronics for T+1 to T+2 quarter earnings disclosure. RH ($40M) and Steve Madden (20-25bp) are calibration points.
